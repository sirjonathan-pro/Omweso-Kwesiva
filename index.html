How to play this version

  1. Player 1 owns the bottom two rows; Player 2 owns the top two rows.
  2. Tap a pit on your side that contains seeds.
  3. Seeds are distributed one at a time around the board in order.
  4. If your last seed lands in an occupied pit, pick up those seeds and continue sowing.
  5. If your last seed lands in an empty pit, your turn ends.
  6. The game ends when a player has no seeds on their side. The player with the most captured seeds wins.
  7. Use SPIN NOW during your turn to earn bonus points.

This is a simplified playable seed-sowing adaptation, not a full implementation of every traditional Omweso rule.
